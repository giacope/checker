Method

Each document is read as numbered lines (PDF text layers, Word, HTML, Markdown, text). For each requirement a model is shown the parts of the documents its search terms find, and answers met, not met, doesn't apply, or open; an answer is kept only when the lines it cites exist and were among those it was shown, and “not met because nothing was found” only when every matching part was shown. Requirements about facts the documents can't show are asked of you. A statement in a document is evidence of what it records, not that it happened. This is an automated assessment, not legal advice.
